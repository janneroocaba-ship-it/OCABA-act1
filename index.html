How to place an Order

To place an order, please contact us through our Facebook page or email us!

  1. Contact us through our Facebook page or email us.
  2. Provide the item(s) you want to order, along with your size and quantity.
  3. We will confirm your order and provide you with the total cost, including shipping fees.
  4. Once you confirm the order, we will provide you with the payment instructions.
  5. After payment is received, we will process and ship your order.
